What these numbers mean
Generation (TWh)
Electricity generated at power stations, the metric the challenge asks for. It is not installed capacity, and not consumption. Consumption-side demand is converted to generation with the observed ratio (1.077 in FY22–26, falling to 1.06 by 2047 as solar and wind have low auxiliary use).
Grid demand vs total
Total adds electricity for green-hydrogen electrolysers (12 Mt at 48 kWh/kg in the base), which is flexible and mostly fed by dedicated renewables.
FY2046-47 ("2047")
India's financial year ending March 2047, the 100th year of independence.
Least-cost mix
A linear programme chooses capacity and dispatch by region in 12 time slices (4 seasons × day/evening/night), subject to resource limits, build-rate limits, transmission, a 15% reserve margin, coal minimum load and a CO₂ cap.
Left out
Bio-power and small hydro (about 2% of generation) are not modelled separately. Rooftop solar is part of solar. Prices are 2026 rupees.
