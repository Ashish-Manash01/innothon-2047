Five questions we would like the mentors' view on

1. Headline CO₂ path: 1.2 Gt or 1.7 Gt in 2047?

1.2 Gt (today's level) gives 79% non-fossil; the draft-NEP-like 1.7 Gt gives 71%. Which is the more defensible base for this jury?

2. Is 12 Mt of green hydrogen the right base?

It is the largest single uncertainty (±~1,000 TWh). Should hydrogen electricity sit inside the 2047 requirement or beside it?

3. Batteries vs pumped storage

At 2026 costs the optimiser picks 573 GW of 4-hour batteries and no new pumped storage; CEA plans 100 GW of pumped storage by 2036. What are we not pricing?

4. Build-speed caps

We cap 2047 at 1,407 GW solar and 412 GW wind (about 82 and 23 GW a year after 2036). Too tight, or too loose?

5. The Eastern region

The East imports 290 TWh and adds 26 GW of nuclear because its sun is weakest and it has no 150 m wind. Realistic?
