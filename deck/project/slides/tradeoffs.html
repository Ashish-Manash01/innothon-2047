Solar 30% dearer changes nothing (it is already build-limited). Building 25% slower overshoots the cap by 615 Mt. Delaying nuclear overshoots it by 105 Mt and brings back imported gas. Building 25% faster cuts cost to ₹3.51. Transmission at 2× instead of 3× barely matters.

The policy message: permitting, land, transmission and manufacturing speed decide the outcome more than technology cost. Nuclear is the flexible lever. A slower build or a delayed nuclear programme pushes India back to coal and imported gas.
