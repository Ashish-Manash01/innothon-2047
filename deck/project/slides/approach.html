Method

Two linked models turn public data into a regional plan

1 · Data

Official sources

CEA, MoP, UN WPP 2024, MoSPI, MoHFW, NISE, NIWE, DAE; 16 automated checks

2 · Demand

State panel model

36 states: income, population, urbanisation; plus EVs, data centres, green H₂

3 · Uncertainty

2,000 Monte Carlo runs

Every driver varied at once; tornado shows which matter

4 · Mix

Least-cost optimiser

5 regions × 6 sources × 12 time slices; resources, build speed, grid, CO₂

5 · Proof

Backcast and benchmarks

Reproduces FY26; checked against CEA, NEP and published 2047 studies

Metric throughout: electricity generated in FY2046-47, not capacity and not consumption. Regions follow CEA's five grids. Every number traces to a source or a reproducible run, live in our dashboard.

The method in one picture. We built two models that hand off to each other: demand by state, rolled up to regions, then a cost-minimising plan for how each region generates it. Step five is the part most teams skip: we tested the plan on today's grid before trusting 2047.
