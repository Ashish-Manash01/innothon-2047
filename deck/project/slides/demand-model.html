Demand model

Electricity use per person rises 0.69% for every 1% rise in income

Estimated, not assumed

State fixed-effects regression, 32 states × 11 years (332 observations). Elasticity 0.689, standard error 0.075, R² 0.986. Four other specifications: 0.663–0.689.

Tested against what happened

Realised FY18→FY26 elasticity was 0.694. The model's FY27 requirement is 1,859 TWh vs CEA's forecast of 1,887 (−1.5%).

Urbanisation works through income

Urban share alone predicts +2.2% use per point, but it vanishes once income is included (correlation 0.71). We keep the term and report it.

Projection: UN population (1,665 million in 2047) × state shares, GDP at 6.5% a year, elasticity easing to 75% of today's by 2047 as demand matures. We tried gradient boosting: worse out of sample (16.3% error vs 9.2%) and it cannot extrapolate.
