Demand model

New loads add about 1,300 TWh that history cannot show

Electric vehicles

459 TWh in 2047. Bottom-up fleet of two-wheelers, cars, three-wheelers, buses and trucks. S-curve calibrated to IEA (~25 TWh in 2030) and IESA (~38 TWh in FY32).

Data centres

250 TWh in 2047. Wood Mackenzie path: 10 TWh in 2025 to 191 TWh in 2040. Placed in announced hubs: Maharashtra, Tamil Nadu, Telangana, Karnataka.

Green hydrogen

588 TWh in 2047: 12 Mt at 48 kWh/kg. Flexible electrolysers, fed mostly by dedicated renewables; kept as a separate layer. Range 5–25 Mt.

Only growth above what the historical trend already contains is added, so nothing is counted twice. These three are the largest source of uncertainty in the forecast.

India 2047 electricity forecast · INNOTHON 2026 · 6

History cannot predict loads that barely exist yet, so we model them bottom-up and calibrate each to a published outlook. Hydrogen alone is about ten percent of 2047 generation, which is why we keep it separate.
