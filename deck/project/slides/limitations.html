Limitations

What the model simplifies

12 slices, not 8,760 hours

Multi-day wind or sun lulls are covered only by the reserve margin. Our storage need is close to CEA's, so the effect looks small.

Short regional history

Region-wise generation is available for FY19–26 and inter-regional flows for FY24–26, so the elasticity is estimated nationally and applied to each region's own income growth.

New loads are outlooks

Hydrogen, EVs and data centres have no history, so they carry wide ranges and dominate the uncertainty.

The official INNOTHON dataset supplies state population and GSDP (GSDP after FY2019-20 is imputed, so MoSPI extends it); its installed-capacity columns are not used, because the rules forbid capacity as a basis. Demand, solar, wind and hydro daily shapes are documented assumptions. Costs are held at 2026 levels, which is conservative for solar and batteries.
