Limitations

What the model simplifies, and what changes on 13 November

12 slices, not 8,760 hours

Multi-day wind or sun lulls are covered only by the reserve margin. Our storage need is close to CEA's, so the effect looks small.

Stylised daily shapes

Demand, solar, wind and hydro profiles are documented assumptions. Hourly data in the official release will replace them.

New loads are outlooks

Hydrogen, EVs and data centres have no history, so they carry wide ranges and dominate the uncertainty.

One script reloads the official datasets, compares them with our public data, and reruns the demand model, Monte Carlo, mix, pathway and dashboard; every change is logged with its cause. Costs are held at 2026 levels, which is conservative for solar and batteries.
