Why the size rose

The registration figure came from a consumption-based state model. The rules require generation only, so we re-fitted on generation: elasticity 0.76 instead of 0.69, and new loads converted with full delivery losses. Result: +9%.

Why the mix held

Higher demand made build speed bind, so the plan builds solar and wind about 10% faster than before (1,407 / 412 GW by 2047). That holds CO₂ at 1.2 Gt and keeps the mix within a point or two of our registration answer.
