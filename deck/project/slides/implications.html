What this means for India

Build speed, not technology cost, decides whether 2047 is clean

Build 70 GW of solar a year

And 20 GW of wind: 1.6× and 3× today's records. Land, permitting and domestic manufacturing are the bottleneck.

Wire the East

Triple inter-regional transfer capacity so the North, West and North-East can supply the East.

Keep nuclear on schedule

80–100 GW is the clean firm power that lets coal plateau; delay brings back imported gas.

Store the sun

About 500 GW of storage by 2047, mostly 4-hour batteries next to solar.
